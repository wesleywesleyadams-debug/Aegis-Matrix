Shields, Armor, Hull

How damage moves through a ship, how the three generalists differ, and what each specialist variant is for. Colors here are the colors in game: shields, armor, hull.

Damage order
SHIELDS → ARMOR → HULL → ship destroyed at 0 hull

Shields

Regenerate on their own for a little energy. Drained inside spice nebulae, so armored fleets own that terrain.

Armor

Never regenerates. Only a Repair Tender restores it, which is what makes support ships matter in a long war.

Hull

The ship's life. A Repair Tender heals hull first, then armor.

Generalists

Each leans on a different layer

Same total toughness per ship, split differently. This is what gives specialists something to specialize against.

Specialists

Five variants of every warship

Color says which layer a variant hits; the marking pattern says which variant it is. Every variant costs the same as its generalist — the price is paid in base damage.

Generalist

100% base

Full damage to shields, armor and hull alike. Never a wrong answer, never a great one.

Shots: yellow lasers

Ion

80% base · ×2.2 shields

Strips shields fast. Best against shield-heavy Destroyers and starbase shields; weak once shields are already down.

Shots: light blue lasers · dotted spine, nose emitter

Breacher

80% base · ×2.2 armor

Chews through plating. Best against armor-heavy Cruisers; wasted on lightly armored swarms.

Shots: red lasers · solid spine, ram prongs

Reaver

80% base · ×1.9 hull

Finishes ships once their outer layers are gone, and mauls hull-heavy corvette swarms and starbases outright.

Shots: green lasers · solid spine, saw teeth

Slip

65% base · ignores shields

Shots pass straight through shields into armor and hull — so shield regeneration never buys the enemy anything. Strongest pick against Destroyers.

Shots: orange V-shots · twin dashed side stripes

Disruptor

40% base · ignores shields + armor

Hits the hull directly, ignoring everything in front of it. Terrible against cheap hulls, decisive against a Cruiser wall.

Shots: green V-shots · V-chevrons down the spine

Cruisers fire torpedoes in the same colors; only lasers take the V shape. Against planets, piercing shots skip the planetary shield, Ion hits it harder, and Reaver kills population faster.

Weapons Depot

Where a generalist becomes a specialist

Building it

250 minerals · 20 spice

A Constructor builds it (E or its panel button) over 30 s. No weapons, and losing it doesn't lose the match.

Refitting

25 minerals · 12 s

Select generalist warships, pick a refit: they fly to the nearest depot, dock, and convert.

Permanent

one way only

A specialist can never be refitted again. Starbases build generalists only, so every specialist passes through a depot.

The AI plays by the same rules: it builds a depot once its fleet grows, then picks refits by working out which variant kills the enemy warships its sensors have actually seen, for the least damage.
