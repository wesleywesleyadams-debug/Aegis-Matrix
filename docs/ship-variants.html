Shields, Armor, Hull

How damage moves through a ship, how the three generalists differ, and what each specialist variant is for. Colors here are the colors in game: shields, armor, hull.

Damage order
SHIELDS → ARMOR → HULL → ship destroyed at 0 hull

Shields

Regenerate on their own for a little energy. Drained inside spice nebulae, so armored fleets own that terrain.

Armor

Never regenerates. Only a Repair Tender restores it, which is what makes support ships matter in a long war.

Hull

The ship's life. A Repair Tender heals hull first, then armor.

Specialists

Five variants of every warship

Shot color says which layer a variant hits. Hull markings are always the owner's color, so the pattern alone names the variant: one line, two ribs, saw teeth, two stripes, chevrons. Every variant costs the same as its generalist — the price is paid in base damage.

Generalist

100% base

Full damage to shields, armor and hull alike. Never a wrong answer, never a great one.

Shots: yellow lasers

Plasma

80% base · ×2.2 shields

Strips shields fast. Best against shield-heavy Destroyers and starbase shields; weak once shields are already down.

Shots: light blue lasers · one solid spine line

Breacher

80% base · ×2.2 armor

Chews through plating. Best against armor-heavy Cruisers; wasted on lightly armored swarms.

Shots: red lasers · two ribs across the hull

Reaver

80% base · ×1.9 hull

Finishes ships once their outer layers are gone, and mauls hull-heavy corvette swarms and starbases outright.

Shots: green lasers · saw teeth along both edges

Slip

65% base · ignores shields

Shots pass straight through shields into armor and hull — so shield regeneration never buys the enemy anything. Strongest pick against Destroyers.

Shots: orange V-shots · two stripes either side of the spine

Disruptor

40% base · ignores shields + armor

Hits the hull directly, ignoring everything in front of it. Terrible against cheap hulls, decisive against a Cruiser wall.

Shots: purple V-shots · a row of V chevrons

Cruisers fire torpedoes in the same colors; only lasers take the V shape. Against planets, piercing shots skip the planetary shield, Plasma hits it harder, and Reaver kills population faster.

Weapons Depots

Five depots, one per variant

Starbases build generalists only. Every specialist is refitted at a depot built for that variant: a Plasma Depot makes Plasma ships, a Slip Depot Slip ships. A depot wears its owner's color and carries its variant's marking pattern.

Building one

250 minerals · 20 spice

A Constructor builds it from the depot buttons in its panel (grid keys Q W E / A S D) over 30 s. No weapons, and losing one doesn't lose the match.

Docking

10 bays

Right-click a depot with generalists selected and they fly in and dock — hidden and safe inside — then refit automatically as minerals allow.

Permanent

one way only

A refitted specialist can never be refitted again, so each ship is committed the moment its refit finishes.

HullRefit costRefit time
Corvette5 minerals15 s
Destroyer10 minerals25 s
Cruiser15 minerals35 s

A refit with no minerals to pay for it waits, and the ship stays inside until you cancel it — cancelling ejects it unchanged, still a generalist. A depot destroyed with ships inside throws them clear, each losing 60% of its shields, armor and hull. The AI builds the depot its intel calls for, then refits by the same kill-cost maths.
